The rules behind every number

  • One unit. One creator delivering one video or one post.
  • One volume. 50 creators in one month, Tony's default. Where a vendor sells packs, the 50-video pack.
  • All in. Subscription or platform fee spread over the 50, plus creator pay, plus every percentage fee or markup.
  • Rights. Whatever the vendor includes. Almost every vendor includes perpetual rights, so Benable is shown twice: $79.99 without paid-ad rights and $179.99 with them.
  • Product out. Gifted product and shipping are left out on both sides. Every platform needs the product.
  • Your time out. Self-serve prices do not include your team's hours. The Who does the work table shows what those hours buy.

Two things decide whether a price compares: do creators post, and are paid-ad rights in. Benable includes the post and sells paid rights per creator. Most rivals include perpetual rights, and the video-file shops don't post at all.
